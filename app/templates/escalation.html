Nothing is sent on a day that is unticked, and nothing is carried over to the next one — the work is still overdue tomorrow and counts a day more, which is exactly what it is. Untick Sunday and an escalation never lands on a head of department's day off, to be read on Monday with everything else or not at all.
0 tells Level 1 on the first day a task is late, which suits a daily checklist and is far too quick for work that takes a week — one late morning reaches a department head. 3 means the ladder starts on the fourth day: Level 1 on day 4, Level 2 on day 5, Level 3 on day 6. The climb is unchanged, a level a day — it just starts later. Work closed before then is never escalated at all, which is the point.
Their work only — delegation, checklist and FMS, counted and split the same three ways. Nobody else is sent it, and it goes from the first day something is late, whatever the grace above: the ladder tells somebody's manager, this tells the person who can actually close the work. With this on, the separate "your work was reported" copy is not sent as well — one email in the morning, not two in the same minute.
