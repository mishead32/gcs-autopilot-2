Escalation finds work somebody was given and has not done. This finds the other failure — a flow that has quietly stopped being followed. No task is overdue, because no task was ever created, so nothing else in the software notices. Counted per flow and per company: an entry at one company does not clear another, which is how a company that has stopped altogether would otherwise hide behind one that has not.
