The two boxes people mix up
After this step, open step number Where the flow goes. The step number to open next. It may be an earlier number — that is a rework loop, and it is allowed. 0 finishes the flow here. Left blank means whatever step comes next in the list.
Start this step's clock from step number When this step is due. Nothing moves anywhere. Blank means the TAT starts counting the moment this step opens. A step number here counts the TAT from that step's planned date instead, so one late step does not drag every deadline after it.
