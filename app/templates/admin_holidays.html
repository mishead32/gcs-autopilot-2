Holidays

Days on which no work is expected, so none is created.

What happens on a holiday
Checklist The rule does not fire that day, and is not owed the next day either. A daily register check on Diwali is simply skipped.
FMS The step's deadline moves to the next working day. The work still has to happen — skipping it would break the chain.
Delegation The same. Set a deadline on a holiday and it moves forward, with a note on the task saying why, instead of marking the doer late for a day nobody was in.
